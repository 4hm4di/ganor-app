Who we are

Ganor (“we”) runs ganor.ng and the Ganor app, and is responsible for the personal data described here. We follow the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

What we collect

When you join the list

Your name, your WhatsApp number or email (you choose), whether you’re a host, a guest or a business, and the kind of event you’re interested in, if you tell us.

When you use Ganor

  • Account: name, phone number, email and password.
  • Tickets: the name and WhatsApp number on each ticket, what you bought and when it was scanned at the gate.
  • Ratings: if you agreed at checkout, the star rating and comment you leave after an event, the name you choose to show, and whether your ticket was scanned.
  • Hosts and businesses: event or business details, contact details and the bank account for payouts.
  • Payments: handled by our licensed payment partners. We receive confirmation of payment, not your card details.

On this website

We don’t use advertising or tracking cookies. The site stores one small note in your browser so the opening animation only plays once per visit.

How we use it

  • To deliver tickets and messages on WhatsApp.
  • To process payments and pay hosts.
  • To check guests in at the gate.
  • To send you one WhatsApp message after an event asking you to rate it, only if you ticked that box at checkout. Reply STOP and we won’t send another.
  • To show guests’ ratings of events and hosts, so people can choose events with confidence.
  • To tell you when Ganor opens, if you joined the list.
  • To keep Ganor safe and stop fraud.
  • To meet our legal duties.

We never sell your personal data.

Who we share it with

  • The host of an event you attend: the name on your ticket, your ticket type and your check-in, so they can run their event. Hosts may only use this for that event.
  • Payment partners: to process payments and payouts.
  • Everyone who visits Ganor: if you leave a rating, your stars, your comment and either your first name and last initial or “Verified attendee” (your choice). Never your phone number. The host can reply publicly.
  • Messaging, email and hosting providers: to send WhatsApp messages and emails, deliver website form submissions to our inbox, and run our service. Some are outside Nigeria, including Supabase (UK), Vercel and Meta (WhatsApp); we use their data protection terms to keep your data safe.
  • Authorities: when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your data only as long as we need it: account data while your account is open, ticket and payment records for as long as the law requires, and waitlist details until Ganor opens or until you ask us to delete them. Rating links and the records of who agreed to rating messages are deleted 6 months after ratings for that event close. Published reviews stay up until you ask us to remove them; they are linked to a coded reference, not your phone number.

Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • Show you the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct it or delete it.
  • Stop using it for messages about Ganor, including rating requests (or just reply STOP).
  • Remove a review you left.
  • Give you a copy to take elsewhere.

Message us and we’ll reply within 30 days. If you’re unhappy with our answer, you can complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

Security

We protect your data with access controls and encryption in transit. If a breach puts your data at risk, we’ll tell you and the Nigeria Data Protection Commission as the law requires.
